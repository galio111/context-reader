
## Imported-document translation cost and selection (2026-10-28)

Status: implemented in an isolated worktree; production acceptance pending.

The galioo Wiley 19-page paper exposed 307 body candidates (63,717 characters), including many landscape-table cells and download-license footers. The revised scope contains 83 body blocks (52,564 characters), retains the abstract and final conclusion, and never mutates the original PDF or stored block IDs. A whole page is classified as a fragmented continued table only when an explicit table marker, continuation marker, at least 30 extracted items, over 90% short items and no long prose all agree. Ordinary fragmented prose is retained.

Document requests remain sequential with one target block, preserving partial cache identities and billing. Paper background is limited to 4,000 source characters / 24 blocks, prioritizing opening definitions and nearby material. The server applies this bound too, removes duplicate target entries from background, and sends background as read-only text without translation IDs. The model must emit target translations only. Short targets no longer receive a 3,600-token minimum output budget. Ordinary article batching is unchanged.

Translation start, headers, body reads and finish waits have finite deadlines. A stalled finish cannot keep a completed/error sidebar loading. Retries retain completed blocks; three no-progress attempts stop. Superseded/owner-replaced streams are cancelled and unknown output IDs cannot enter caches. Existing exact document-block caches remain authoritative over article aggregates.

PDF selection endpoints remain at the last valid text hit in blank gutters/margins; native caret snapping cannot jump to a distant DOM run. Mouse release outside the document completes the existing selection, and window blur clears a pending drag. Word edges still expand after deliberate selection. PDF/Word containers use compact outer padding/full shell width; native source margins/layout are unchanged. Desktop retains one rail Back action, mobile its toolbar Back action. Actions during account restoration show a short restoration notice rather than opening Login.

Local evidence: 169 architecture checks, 90 critical checks, 24 focused account/selection/timeout checks, release safety and 11 protected contracts pass. Edge and WebKit, using the real 1,467,198-byte paper with the actual DocumentReader component, retain the same selection while moving along an empty margin and submit the completed words on outside mouse release; no page errors. These local fixture checks are not public-site or physical Mac Safari acceptance.

Three provider samples (abstract, hydrogen-bond prose, final conclusion) returned only their requested IDs, preserved chemical terms and source quantities, and stopped normally. Total 3,167 input / 735 output tokens, 0.937–2.298 s, internal pricing estimate CNY 0.010708. Naive 83-request extrapolation is CNY 0.2963; this is not a complete-paper measured bill, cap or supplier invoice. Full regeneration was not run. The original 307-block job was cancelled at the owner's explicit request through existing quota settlement; completed caches were not deleted. Already-dispatched work may finish, but the closed action cannot authorize later blocks.

Private source PDFs, article bodies, tokens, exact translated content and raw usage logs remain outside Git.
