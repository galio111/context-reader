## Quantity, balance and budget

Each of current affairs, science, culture and business must exceed 13 publications (minimum 14) per Shanghai day. Neither categories nor the total has a publication ceiling. Publish every eligible approved candidate; quantity satisfaction never stops further work. Deficits rank sources first, but do not discard other sources. The CNY 1 soft stop is removed. The existing CNY 1.50 daily reservation cap, 240 attempts and 120-minute paid-processing window remain. If a boundary is reached, undispatched work stays in the durable queue; no guarantee of sufficient daily quality supply is made. School reading remains paused for automatic publication and manual workflows remain available.

## Configurable combined audit

The server-owned model routes choose editorial primary/fallback and an independent vision route. If both editorial choices support images, a combined text/image/classification call avoids duplicate work. If either is text-only, one text call and one vision call are required. The code default remains DeepSeek Flash without fallback; current production configuration selects DeepSeek Flash then MiMo V2.6 Flash for both routes. Provider failures, including HTTP 200 responses without usable completion or token usage, may invoke the configured backup once within the same persistent daily budget. An unknown-use request retains its conservative reservation. Input/policy failures and incomplete streamed responses are not bypassed by switching vendors.

Jev is independently disabled by default and uses the official TypeSafe endpoint with TYPESAFE_API_KEY. When enabled, a single typed request evaluates four text defects: incomplete, contamination, mediaDependent and promotional. Probabilities <=0.10 or >=0.90 are decisive; intermediate values defer only the uncertain checks to the main model. A decisive defect stops before the expensive generative audit. Confident clean checks are omitted from the main-model output schema and merged in code. No image decisions, summary writing or complex linguistic difficulty are delegated to Jev. Source completeness and deterministic checks remain required. These thresholds are routing policy, not measured accuracy; live Jev quality cannot be claimed until actual-key testing.

Deterministic extraction, minimum 401 English words, original-page completeness comparison, readable and fully stored images, source date and duplicate checks remain mandatory. Audit policy v3 additionally requires sourceCompletenessVerified, exact content hash and an approval under 48 hours. Storage may change image URLs but not the audited text/block projection. Exact-content passed audit cache lasts strictly less than 48 hours, matching approval expiry. Held same-content decisions stay held for seven days. Cached evidence blocks expired news before another paid audit; content/policy changes invalidate reuse. Cache cannot approve edited content or replace original-page, image and freshness checks.

Automatic publication checks the finalized article immediately before changing `published`: at least 401 English words, at least one non-presentation body image stored in the first-party cover bucket, and a first-party recommendation cover URL must all survive localization and sanitization. On 2026-09-23, all 55 new articles had distinct cover URLs and all 55 original cover URLs returned HTTP 200. The two published rows without any cover are older NPR articles from 2026-08-13/14; neither belongs to that day's run. HTTP availability cannot prove the browser painted every card promptly; the Reader image-scroll issue remains open.

## Scheduling and accounting

Database lease serializes bounded source batches. Up to three URL attempts per batch; obvious videos/films/APOD/shorts are filtered before occupying paid slots. Eligible, already-paid v3 candidates are published first. Verified non-lower sources rotate with category/difficulty priority; at most six visits per wave. Two consecutive empty batches retire a source, except inspected WordPress `/feed` and `/feed/rss` sources may continue to their sixth bounded archive page. Identical held content is not automatically re-reviewed.

The source policy first spends attempts on categories below 13; it then uses categories with room to approach 60; current affairs has no upper cap. Proven secondary sources remain eligible alongside primary sources. Expired prior approvals in deficient categories are reimported and fully audited in place once per day before additional feed discovery, preserving their ids and manual edits. It ranks eligible sites using actual category output from reviewed articles over the previous 72 hours. A site with at least eight reviewed outputs and none in a currently deficient category is skipped for that deficit, even if its declared topics include it. Current limits are 240 URL attempts, 120 minutes and a CNY 1.50 hard budget. Exhausted supply or another hard stop reports its specific reason. The three-day production audit found different causes, including an unknown-use reserve budget stop on 28 September and source exhaustion on 27 and 29 September.

The Guardian uses verified Business, Economics, Companies, Markets and Money feeds; World remains excluded. The 5 October production verification is recorded in editorial-efficiency-20261005.md. CNBC now has Business and Economy feeds; the Economy probe yielded two complete, readable, image-bearing samples and one timeout. The Conversation Business uses its AU, US and UK regional feeds: nine of nine sampled articles passed production-network full-text, ordered-block and image-readability checks, replacing its earlier mainland image-failure status. Inc. (403), Fortune (roughly 100-word extracts), OECD Ecoscope (insufficient cadence), New Atlas (gallery-like image counts) and CNBC Energy (failed verification) remain disabled. Readability probes leave time for fallback (first attempt 5 seconds, fallback 12 seconds under the 20-second probe); body image downloads and stored quality retain existing limits. Source expansion improves opportunity; it does not prove that 14 suitable commercial articles exist every day.

The systemd driver processes running batches after 2 seconds and polls again 30 seconds after inactivity. An independent hourly feed sweep captures all enabled, verified sources in batches of three, including after the paid day closes and before 06:00; it spends no model tokens. The cursor prevents repeated reads on each cron tick. Quantity no longer stops a day. Paid work stops at the shared hard budget, 120 minutes, attempt limit, source exhaustion or repeated provider/schema failures. Already-paid eligible candidates publish first without category ceilings. Duplicate/stale publication failures are isolated. Source exhaustion can receive one later supply check within the existing same-day allowance, even when the category minimums are already met; hard boundaries and suspension do not restart paid work. Accepted report identities retain the historical `_60_` key for SMTP idempotency only; it is not a quantity limit. Hourly discovery continues to preserve work for the next review day.

Durable pre-request reservation and per-request journal include article URL/hash, model, stage, timing, HTTP status, cached/input/output tokens, settled estimated CNY/USD, unknown reserve and final audit outcome. Invalid JSON or business validation cannot erase settled usage. Batch outcomes are append-only by source/visit, not overwritten. New request records distinguish scheduled tasks, current-day recovery and backlog processing; reports separate these phases while sharing one budget. Historical unlabelled requests remain unlabelled. Estimates remain distinct from provider invoices.

Emails include actual publication count, category/topic/difficulty counts, attempts, all pipeline spending, provider/stage totals, tokens and unknown reserves. No Jev accuracy claim is made. Quality, future supply and model accuracy are never guaranteed by passing unit tests alone.

## Missed-article protection

Discovery stores every feed item before article processing. Queue states distinguish waiting, technical retry, needs inspection, content rejection, policy skip and candidate created. Unknown exceptions are technical, with stage and reason. Network/image/model-format failures retry after one hour then six hours; three failed attempts remain visible for inspection. Structural uncertainty and capacity limits go directly to inspection. No unresolved entry expires when it leaves RSS. Resolved decisions are retained for 30 days; at 5,000 retained entries per source, intake reports a capacity fault and never silently evicts unresolved work. This is a storage guard, not a publication quota. Admin can explicitly requeue technical items; publication gates still run.

Short extraction alone does not prove a short original article, so results below 401 words are held for inspection, rather than labelled low quality. Incomplete/contaminated text or orphan captions are extraction concerns, not automatic editorial rejection. Confident advertising, media-dependent material or an unsuitable substantive reading purpose can be recorded as content rejection. Old news, duplicates, non-English or paused difficulty are separately labelled policy skips. Known URLs are deduplicated after reload; title similarity alone does not discard a new report. Old transient per-day excluded URLs do not override the durable queue.

Top-N titles and substantive rankings are audited on their actual content. Integrated audit v3 allows up to 24 images (matching extraction) while retaining complete ordered images, 30 MB image-download budget, bounded text, paid reservation and provider limits. Larger work is held for inspection; no text or pictures are sampled away to approve it. Publisher rules remain deterministic, narrowly scoped and regression-tested. Failure evidence and shape profiles inform repairs; the system never executes model-written regex or weakens review automatically.

Source Admin displays the cumulative queue counts, recent technical reasons/retry actions and recent rejection reasons. Daily reports distinguish queue health from meeting the quantity floor. A successful feed check does not prove complete extraction of every article; a successful day does not prove every publisher was reachable. Rights-disabled, cadence-unverified and manually disabled sources remain disabled.
