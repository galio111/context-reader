## Behavior

Admin > batch intake > automatic editorial has an independent publication switch. The existing schedule switch/time still controls scheduled discovery. Both must be enabled for unattended publication. At final acceptance both are enabled, with the existing 06:00 Asia/Shanghai discovery schedule and DeepSeek provider; the daily attempt limit is 90. Manual single-source runs and manual imports keep the candidate workflow. The target counts newly auto-published articles on the Shanghai calendar day, rather than raw candidate inventory or manual publications. High-school/CET-4 and lower labels remain available but are excluded from this pipeline; lower-source feeds are skipped. Domestic examination acquisition is deferred.

Every candidate must contain at least 401 English words, pass source/date/duplicate/English/quality rules, and retain all accepted body images through bounded safe storage. The extractor compares its selected body with an independent article-body candidate when available, recording missing long text blocks and images. This is a structural signal, not proof of perfect extraction: a page with no independent body container can still hide omissions. Full-text model review complements the comparison.

DeepSeek Flash classifies the full text and assesses five defects: missing content, non-body contamination, captions without images, dependence on video/audio, and promotion. Uncertain difficulty/classification and every proposed school-level classification escalate to Pro; uncertain/defective text decisions receive Pro review. All retained actual images are checked in batches of three using deepseek-flash, including a second vision-capable check for doubts. V4 Pro is text-only and must never receive image review requests. Conflicting image decisions remain held. Any incomplete response, unresolved uncertainty or failed image check prevents publication. Long text is reviewed without head/tail sampling, bounded to 12 chunks; over-limit content is held. Reviews have a 3-minute soft deadline, checked before each bounded provider call. A content hash binds title, text and ordered blocks. Publication re-reads the candidate, checks the hash and uses updated_at compare-and-swap so later edits cannot inherit approval.

The existing seven topic tags, three adult difficulty labels and source diversity drive selection; the four homepage presentation categories are retained. Each day caps a source at 4 articles, a primary topic at 6 and a difficulty at 12; seven-day deficits receive priority. These are balancing constraints, not a guarantee of an exact uniform distribution. Insufficient supply leaves an explicit shortfall rather than relabelling or lowering quality. Each batch reserves at most three attempts before network work; default daily limit is 90 (Admin range 30–240). A shared database lease serializes runs. A pending-curation journal repairs a crash between publication and homepage placement. Featured selection uses reservoir randomness without another AI call.

Images, captions, quotes, tables and mixed substantive blocks are protected; retained prose is unchanged. Original/repaired hashes and exact removed blocks remain in review metadata. Every repaired article is classified again and receives the full text/image publication gates.
