# Automated editorial publication

As of 2026-09-20, the initial pipeline is deployed through release 20260920T000500, but automatic publication is paused for acceptance debugging and the existing-library audit. The JSONB hash fix and further audit safety changes are pending deployment. A full 30-article day remains unverified.

## Behavior

Admin > batch intake > automatic editorial has an independent publication switch. The existing schedule switch/time still controls scheduled discovery. Both must be enabled for unattended publication. Manual single-source runs and manual imports keep the candidate workflow. The target counts newly auto-published articles on the Shanghai calendar day, never old candidates or manual publications. High-school/CET-4 and lower labels remain available but are excluded from this pipeline; lower-source feeds are skipped. Domestic examination acquisition is deferred.

Every candidate must contain at least 401 English words, pass source/date/duplicate/English/quality rules, and retain all accepted body images through bounded safe storage. The extractor compares its selected body with an independent article-body candidate when available, recording missing long text blocks and images. This is a structural signal, not proof of perfect extraction: a page with no independent body container can still hide omissions. Full-text model review complements the comparison.

DeepSeek Flash classifies the full text and assesses five defects: missing content, non-body contamination, captions without images, dependence on video/audio, and promotion. Uncertain difficulty/classification and every proposed school-level classification escalate to Pro; uncertain/defective text decisions receive Pro review. All retained actual images are checked in batches of three using deepseek-flash, including a second vision-capable check for doubts. V4 Pro is text-only and must never receive image review requests. Conflicting image decisions remain held. Any incomplete response, unresolved uncertainty or failed image check prevents publication. Long text is reviewed without head/tail sampling, bounded to 12 chunks; over-limit content is held. Reviews have a 3-minute soft deadline, checked before each bounded provider call. A content hash binds title, text and ordered blocks. Publication re-reads the candidate, checks the hash and uses updated_at compare-and-swap so later edits cannot inherit approval.

The existing seven topic tags, three adult difficulty labels and source diversity drive selection; the four homepage presentation categories are retained. Each day caps a source at 4 articles, a primary topic at 6 and a difficulty at 12; seven-day deficits receive priority. These are balancing constraints, not a guarantee of an exact uniform distribution. Insufficient supply leaves an explicit shortfall rather than relabelling or lowering quality. Each batch reserves at most three attempts before network work; default daily limit is 90 (Admin range 30–150). A shared database lease serializes runs. A pending-curation journal repairs a crash between publication and homepage placement. Featured selection uses reservoir randomness without another AI call.

## Provider and budget

Default is DeepSeek. Optional `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` enables `jev-shadow`: AI SDK experimental_evaluate, model typesafe-ai/jev, five boolean probability questions over the same complete text. Jev remains advisory and does not replace DeepSeek approval until calibrated against labelled examples. Missing keys, budget exhaustion, invalid probabilities, API errors and timeouts leave DeepSeek active. There is no working Jev credential in this acceptance run, so live Jev accuracy, account entitlement and billing are unverified.

The configurable Jev monthly reserve is at most $4, leaving headroom relative to an eligible $5 Gateway credit. Reservation is conservative at the researched $0.042/M input-token rate, before each request, and is not refunded on timeout. It covers this pipeline only, not other Gateway projects; pricing or account changes require revisiting the rate. It does not establish that an account receives free credit or does not need a card. Direct DeepSeek works independently of Vercel billing. Runtime model overrides: EDITORIAL_DEEPSEEK_MODEL (deepseek-flash), EDITORIAL_DEEPSEEK_REVIEW_MODEL (deepseek-v4-pro). Real credentials belong only in ignored private runtime environments.

Bounded DeepSeek samples after cleanup: Nautilus moon/crater 415 words, review $0.000419; mastodon 429 words, $0.000504; Noema long AI essay, $0.001530; Noema AI commentary 1,220 words, $0.000752. These numbers cover text/image editorial review only, excluding classification, rejected attempts, transport/storage and hosting. Cache/peak pricing changes totals. Planning allowance for 900 monthly accepted articles plus rejected attempts/classification is $6–10/month for AI, approximately $0.20–0.33/day; this is not a measured full-month bill or a guaranteed spending cap. Jev shadow adds calls rather than saving DeepSeek cost during calibration.

## Source evidence

`verify-editorial-sources.ts` uses actual robots, feed, overseas-fetch, shared extraction and bounded image-readability paths. Local artifacts retain complete samples and original HTML; they are ignored and not published. Noema: three complete readable samples, author biography removed. Nautilus: two over-400-word readable samples passed DeepSeek; a third was 380 words and rejected. Quanta: readable long-form samples, but specialist and podcast filters still apply. SAPIENS is too stale; Greater Good samples include video pages; Atlas Obscura returns 403; The Marginalian image reads fail; MIT Press Reader robots cannot be verified. These are observations at the test time, not permanent judgements. Defaults are disabled. Existing database source registries require explicit save/verify/enable through Admin; adding a default does not modify a live registry.

## Validation and limits

17 editorial regressions cover full-text coverage, strict boolean schema, label exclusions, content binding, rejected/edited candidates, image structure, balance, sanitizer cleanup, Jev outage and budget-store fallback, DeepSeek failure and Pro escalation. 89 existing critical and 18 discovery regressions plus protected release contracts passed. Desktop/mobile form interaction was exercised with browser API fixtures; live Admin authorization/publication must be checked separately after deployment. No model can certify perfect completeness or objectively exact learner difficulty. Retained manual review is the recovery path.

## Existing-library audit acceptance

The user authorized retaining qualified published articles, publishing qualified candidates and deleting confirmed defective articles, only after debugging the system. Existing qualified school-level published articles stay; qualified school-level candidates stay unpublished. Old labels are not evidence: review full text, confirm proposed low difficulty with Pro, and retain uncertainty rather than assert a low level. No personal saved articles or vocabulary are part of this audit.

Private server snapshots on 2026-09-20 contain 240 candidates, 233 published articles, 55 previously rejected candidates and homepage curation. `scripts/audit-editorial-snapshot.ts` is read-only and resumable. A completed audit and explicit confirmed defects are distinct from an interrupted request or uncertainty. These fields alone do not authorize deletion: destructive processing must also verify fresh row identity and the captured evidence. Initial pilot results are validation material, not completed cleanup. Source snapshots, model reports and credentials are ignored artifacts and must not be committed.
