Difficulty is a ranking preference. School reading stays excluded from automatic publications; manual workflows remain available.

## Configurable combined audit

The server-owned model routes choose editorial primary/fallback and an independent vision route. If both editorial choices support images, a combined text/image/classification call avoids duplicate work. If either is text-only, one text call and one vision call are required. The code default remains DeepSeek Flash without fallback; current production configuration selects DeepSeek Flash then MiMo V2.6 Flash for both routes. Provider failures, including HTTP 200 responses without usable completion or token usage, may invoke the configured backup once within the same persistent daily budget. An unknown-use request retains its conservative reservation. Input/policy failures and incomplete streamed responses are not bypassed by switching vendors.

Jev is independently disabled by default and uses the official TypeSafe endpoint with TYPESAFE_API_KEY. When enabled, a single typed request evaluates four text defects: incomplete, contamination, mediaDependent and promotional. Probabilities <=0.10 or >=0.90 are decisive; intermediate values defer only the uncertain checks to the main model. A decisive defect stops before the expensive generative audit. Confident clean checks are omitted from the main-model output schema and merged in code. No image decisions, summary writing or complex linguistic difficulty are delegated to Jev. Source completeness and deterministic checks remain required. These thresholds are routing policy, not measured accuracy; live Jev quality cannot be claimed until actual-key testing.

Deterministic extraction, minimum 401 English words, original-page completeness comparison, readable and fully stored images, source date and duplicate checks remain mandatory. Audit policy v3 additionally requires sourceCompletenessVerified, exact content hash and an approval under 48 hours. Storage may change image URLs but not the audited text/block projection. Exact-content audit cache lasts 24 hours; it cannot approve edited content or replace freshness checks.

Automatic publication checks the finalized article immediately before changing `published`: at least 401 English words, at least one non-presentation body image stored in the first-party cover bucket, and a first-party recommendation cover URL must all survive localization and sanitization. On 2026-09-23, all 55 new articles had distinct cover URLs and all 55 original cover URLs returned HTTP 200. The two published rows without any cover are older NPR articles from 2026-08-13/14; neither belongs to that day's run. HTTP availability cannot prove the browser painted every card promptly; the Reader image-scroll issue remains open.

## Extraction experience

Versioned publisher rules remove inspected standalone furniture: ScienceAlert subscription artwork, Nieman posted/share/footer boundary, Popular Science related-module heading and column promotion. Rules are host-scoped, preserve quotes/captions/tables and require substantive content after removal. No model-generated regex or arbitrary prose deletion is executed. Unrecognized contamination remains pending; it is not repeatedly sent to Pro.

The source/ordered-block-template profile records distinct successful URLs and failures; five distinct clean samples mark the profile verified, any held sample resets it. Profiles support future rule maintenance and expose recurring failures. They do NOT currently waive the original-page comparison or integrated content check: source layout can change, and the combined check adds no extra model request. Do not advertise this as an autonomous selector-learning engine or permanent exemption from quality checks.

## Scheduling and accounting

Database lease serializes bounded source batches. Up to three URL attempts per batch; obvious videos/films/APOD/shorts are filtered before occupying paid slots. Eligible, already-paid v3 candidates are published first. Verified non-lower sources rotate with category/difficulty priority; at most six visits. Two consecutive empty batches retire a source, except inspected WordPress `/feed` and `/feed/rss` sources may continue to their sixth bounded archive page. Identical held content is not automatically re-reviewed.

The source policy first spends attempts on categories below 13; it then uses categories with room to approach 60; current affairs has no upper cap. Proven secondary sources remain eligible alongside primary sources. Expired prior approvals in deficient categories are reimported and fully audited in place once per day before additional feed discovery, preserving their ids and manual edits. It ranks eligible sites using actual category output from reviewed articles over the previous 72 hours. A site with at least eight reviewed outputs and none in a currently deficient category is skipped for that deficit, even if its declared topics include it. Current limits are 240 URL attempts, 120 minutes and a CNY 1.50 hard budget. Exhausted supply or another hard stop reports its specific reason. The three-day production audit found different causes, including an unknown-use reserve budget stop on 28 September and source exhaustion on 27 and 29 September.

The Guardian uses only its Business feed; its former World feed produced many current-affairs candidates. CNBC now has Business and Economy feeds; the Economy probe yielded two complete, readable, image-bearing samples and one timeout. The Conversation Business uses its AU, US and UK regional feeds: nine of nine sampled articles passed production-network full-text, ordered-block and image-readability checks, replacing its earlier mainland image-failure status. Inc. (403), Fortune (roughly 100-word extracts), OECD Ecoscope (insufficient cadence), New Atlas (gallery-like image counts) and CNBC Energy (failed verification) remain disabled. Source expansion improves opportunity; it does not prove that 13 suitable commercial articles exist every day.

The systemd driver immediately processes the next running batch after 2 seconds, yields after a bounded session, and the timer resumes 30 seconds after inactivity. New paid discovery stops at 60 with balanced categories; already-eligible current affairs continues without an upper limit. Other stopping boundaries are CNY 1 after the full 55/category minimum, hard budget, 120 minutes, attempt limit, source exhaustion, three provider/schema failures in sequence, or exhausted eligible sources. No-progress batches remain logged but do not end a day while an unmet category still has untried sources. An individual duplicate/stale publication is journalled and skipped instead of blocking the entire pool. Unexpected fatal failures close the day, retain private details and request a shortfall email. Email status is read from the current day’s durable record. Every stopped outcome requests a completion/shortfall email; failed email delivery can retry without restarting paid work.

Durable pre-request reservation and per-request journal include article URL/hash, model, stage, timing, HTTP status, cached/input/output tokens, settled estimated CNY/USD, unknown reserve and final audit outcome. Invalid JSON or business validation cannot erase settled usage. Batch outcomes are append-only by source/visit, not overwritten. Estimates remain distinct from provider invoices.

Emails include actual publication count, category/topic/difficulty counts, attempts, all pipeline spending, provider/stage totals, tokens and unknown reserves. No Jev accuracy claim is made. Quality, future supply and model accuracy are never guaranteed by passing unit tests alone.

## Inventory and explicit recovery

Public summaries, published inventory and candidates page through REST in stable 200-row pages; the backend 1000-row ceiling must not truncate deduplication or the catalogue. The bounded 10000-entry selection-date map orders active/recent selections first, independent of JSONB key order. Missing dates are restored from the original `autoPublishedAt`, never from the repair time.

Admin-only same-origin POST `/api/admin/editorial` accepts `resume_today`, `publish_approved` with 1–3 candidate ids, or final `report_recovery`. All actions share the discovery lease. Resume retains existing costs, attempts, source visits and original start time; only the unused part of the 120-minute processing window is restored with a private recovery journal. Bulk publication requires passed status, current v3 hash/completeness/image/date gates, and reimports expired approvals under the shared daily budget. Held, unaudited, stale and quota-overflow rows are retained with reasons. No crawler intake publishes without the editorial gate. Receipts distinguish published, duplicate and retained candidates.

Publisher display clocks with punctuated a.m./p.m. are recognized for freshness without changing their stored original date. Stale/future/missing dates still fail. Recovery publication also carries autoPublishedAt and contributes to the day. A final explicit recovery report reconciles all selected-today publications and sends one idempotent updated email after bulk processing; repeating the action does not send it again.
